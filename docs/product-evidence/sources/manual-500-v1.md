# Metro 水杯 · Manual

## 1 | Product overview

Metro 水杯 is a reusable insulated drink bottle for everyday water, commuting and short walks. The package includes a bottle, a screw cap, a removable seal, and this booklet. The bottle uses passive insulation. Actual beverage temperature depends on fill level, ambient conditions and how often the cap is opened.

## 2 | Before first use

Rinse each part before using the bottle for the first time. Remove the shipping insert from the cap. A new seal can have a mild packaging smell. Wash the parts with mild soap and let them dry completely before assembly.

## 3 | Exterior cleaning

Wipe painted surfaces with a soft cloth and mild detergent. Use no bleach, scouring powder or metal brushes. Dry the outer finish before putting it into a fabric sleeve.

## 4 | Body material

The inner liner that touches the drink is 304 stainless steel. The outer shell is also stainless steel and has a powder coating. The liner contains no painted surface.

## 5 | Bottle body wash

The bottle body is dishwasher safe on the upper rack only, using a cycle no hotter than 60 °C. Remove the screw cap and silicone seal before loading. The dry cycle may be used only when its stated temperature stays within the same limit. Keep the body away from exposed heating elements and leave space between the body and neighboring utensils.

## 6 | Cap wash

Wash the cap by hand in warm soapy water. Do not put the screw cap or its removable silicone seal in a dishwasher, even if the bottle body is in the same load. Rinse the cap threads after cleaning and allow the cap to air dry before reassembly.

## 7 | Drying and storage

Store with the lid off when the bottle is dry. Keep away from solvents, excessive humidity and direct heat. Do not nest wet components inside the bottle during storage.

## 8 | Filling

Use the fill mark below the thread. Do not fill the bottle up to the rim. Close the lid completely before carrying it and keep it upright in a bag.

## 9 | Beverages

This bottle is intended for still water. Do not put carbonated beverages or dry ice in the sealed bottle. Pressure can build during storage and cause the cap to release suddenly.

## 10 | Heating

Never heat any part in a microwave or oven. Keep the bottle off a stovetop. Do not pour liquids directly from a rolling boil into the liner.

## 11 | Cold storage

Do not freeze the bottle with liquid inside. For chilled water, add cold water or small ice cubes by hand. Wait for the liner to warm to room temperature before cleaning.

## 12 | Opening the cap

Hold the body upright and turn the cap counterclockwise. If the cap feels stuck, let the bottle reach room temperature and open it over a sink.

## 13 | Refitting the seal

Fit the silicone seal with its flat side facing the cap and the raised edge facing the bottle. Check that the ring lies flat all the way around the groove before use.

## 14 | Leak inspection

If the bottle drips, inspect the groove for debris and check the seal for twists. Refit the cap and test with still water over a sink. Retire any cap with a damaged thread.

## 15 | Travel and fit

Measure the holder opening at its narrowest point. Allow enough clearance for the full diameter and leave room above to lift the bottle straight out.

## 16 | Drop inspection

A dent at the base can prevent the bottle from standing flat. Do not use a bottle with a sharp rim or a cracked lid. A cosmetic scuff in the paint can be wiped clean.

## 17 | Coating and labels

The underside identification label is intended to stay on the bottle. Rubbing the painted shell with aggressive cleaner may dull the color. The carton artwork can differ by market.

## 18 | Replacement cap matching

Order a replacement cap by the part reference printed in the specification table. Similar looking caps can use different threads. Do not force mismatched parts together.

## 19 | Packing list

Each retail box contains one body, one cap and one fitted seal. The paper booklet is packed under the insert. A carrying sleeve and spare seal are sold separately.

## 20 | 运输提示

运输前排空杯中液体，将杯身和杯盖擦干。长途运输时使用外盒保护杯口，避免杯口承受侧向冲击。

## 21 | 拆装提示

取下密封圈前先冲洗盖内沟槽。只用手指提起密封圈。不得用刀片、针或其他尖锐工具撬动密封圈。

## 22 | 气味处理

如长期存放后有气味，先用温水和中性洗涤剂清洗，再分开晾干。不要把香精或有机溶剂加进杯中。

## 23 | Disposal

Separate the cap and gasket from the steel body before disposal. Follow the local rules for metal, mixed plastic and silicone items.

## 24 | Quality record

The underside batch code identifies the production lot. Read it under good light when contacting support. Retail packaging barcodes identify a stock item instead of a production lot.
