# Metro 水杯 · FAQ

## ice | Ice cubes

Q: Can I add ice cubes?
A: Small ice cubes may be added by hand. Never force them past the neck or use dry ice.

## scratch | Surface mark

Q: Does a scratch outside affect the liner?
A: An external paint scuff alone does not contact the drink. Stop use if the rim has a sharp edge or the lid is cracked.

## pressure | Pressure in a closed bottle

Q: Can I use sparkling water?
A: Use still water only. Carbonated drinks can build pressure with the cap closed.

## heat | Warming a drink

Q: May I warm a drink in the bottle?
A: No. The bottle must stay out of the microwave, oven and off the stovetop.

## dish | Loading the dishwasher

Q: Do the body and cap follow the same wash instructions?
A: See the Bottle body wash and Cap wash pages before loading. The parts have separate cleaning requirements.

## seal | 拧紧后仍渗漏

Q: 拧紧后还有水滴怎么办？
A: 检查盖内沟槽和密封圈是否扭曲，清洁并重新装配后，在水槽上方用静水试漏。螺纹损坏时停用。

## share | Cross-fitting caps

Q: Will a similar cap fit?
A: Check the replacement part number in the spec table for your exact SKU. Appearance alone is insufficient.

## chill | Temperature experience

Q: What affects the temperature inside?
A: Temperature depends on the starting temperature, how full the bottle is, the room temperature and how frequently it is opened. Cool the beverage before filling if you prefer it cold.

## sleeve | 包装内的附件

Q: 随箱附带杯套或备用密封圈吗？
A: 杯套和备用密封圈需要另购。零售盒随附一只已装配的密封圈。

## smell | 气味处理

Q: 可以用香精去味吗？
A: 请用中性洗涤剂清洗并分开晾干，杯内不要加香精。

## recycle | Sorting components

Q: How should I dispose of the bottle?
A: Separate the steel body, screw cap and removable silicone gasket. Follow local waste rules for each component.

## rim | 瓶口保护

Q: 运输时怎样保护杯口？
A: 排空并擦干后，用外盒保护杯口，避免侧向冲击。

## smallparts | Handling spare parts

Q: Can I leave spare seals on a table?
A: Keep spare seals out of reach of children. Small loose parts can be swallowed.

## brush | Cleaning brushes

Q: What should I use to scrub the outside?
A: Use a soft cloth. Never scrub the painted shell with metal brushes or scouring powder.

## fill | How much to fill

Q: Can I fill it right to the rim?
A: Use the fill mark under the screw threads so the cap can be closed without displacing water.

## carry | Carrying a filled bottle

Q: Can it be carried sideways in a bag?
A: Close the cap fully and carry the bottle upright.
