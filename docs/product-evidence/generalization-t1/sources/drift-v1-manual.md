## 1 | Packing
Fold the hose against the handle. Leave the pump vents uncovered until the outside is dry.

## 2 | Working water temperature
Use water at or below 42 °C at the pump inlet during spraying.

## 3 | Priming reservoir
Add clean water before priming. Point the nozzle into a drain until the spray is steady.

## 4 | Electrical compartment
Do not immerse the motor housing. Dry the outside before plugging in the charger.

## 5 | Handle
Carry the reservoir by the recessed handle with its lid closed. Avoid lifting by the hose.

## 6 | Maintenance
Check the hose for flattening and kinks. Hang it loosely between uses.

## 7 | Continuous spray duty cycle
Spray for no more than 12 minutes continuously, then leave the pump off for 15 minutes.

## 8 | Filter receptacle
Shut the pump off and remove the inlet basket before inspecting the screen.

## 9 | Pressure relief
Press the trigger into a drain after powering off to relieve pressure in the hose.

## 10 | Fill height
Keep the reservoir below the fill shoulder. Leave air above the water line.

## 11 | Shipping
Empty and vent the reservoir before transportation. Clip the nozzle into the holder.

## 12 | Detachable caddy
The detachable cleaning caddy can be rinsed in water up to 65 °C with no motor attached.

## 13 | Rinse procedure
Run clear water through the reservoir before packing. Charge only when the outside is dry.

## 14 | Nozzle drying
Air-dry the spray head overnight. Do not force tools through the nozzle holes.

## 15 | Power supply care
Connect the charger to an indoor outlet. Pull the plug before wiping the adapter.

## 16 | Drain additive
After disconnecting the nozzle, use a 1% citric acid solution for the reservoir descaling cycle.

## 17 | Cold-storage preparation
For winter storage, remove the inlet basket before putting the empty pump in its tray.
