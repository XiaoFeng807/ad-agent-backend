## 1 | Deployment
Remove the transit tab. Open the stand until it rests against its hinge stop.

## 2 | Battery indicator
Three green bars indicate the main light battery is above 80%. A single flashing bar means recharge soon.

## 3 | Charging inlet
Charge from a 5 V USB-C supply. Close the dust cover before setting the lamp on a dusty bench.

## 4 | Mounting
Seat the magnetic base on a flat, unpainted steel surface. Check that the lamp does not slide before letting go.

## 5 | Mounting magnet
The magnetic base is a workshop mount. A weak pull on a narrow steel edge is expected; reposition it.

## 6 | Lens care
Wipe the lens with a dry microfiber cloth. Do not use solvent on the optical window.

## 7 | Low-brightness mode
Tap the dimmer once from off for the 90 lumen bench mode, or tap again to move to high.

## 8 | Splash protection
Only the closed lamp enclosure has an IPX4 splash rating. Light rain is permitted with the USB cover closed.

## 9 | Storage
Store indoors in a dry crate. Keep the hinge clear of packing material and protect the lens from loose tools.

## 10 | Folding stand
Fold the stand flush against the back after use. Check for trapped cables near the hinge.

## 11 | USB input temperature
Charge the built-in light battery between 5 and 35 °C. Let the unit cool before connecting power.

## 12 | Battery servicing
The built-in lamp cell is not supplied as a loose field spare. Arrange service through an authorized workshop.
